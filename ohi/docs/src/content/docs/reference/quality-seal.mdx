We award exceptionally well written and maintained ontologies with quality seals to help you choose
when several ontologies match your needs.

<QualitySeals />

On [ohi.dev](https://ohi.dev), the seal appears beside the ontology's title and as a small badge in
Search and Browse.

## What we consider

We use quality measures to encourage and reward ontology maintainers who follow best practices to
improve quality and usability.

Quality scoring is still experimental and heavily subject to change. We currently consider:

- **Term descriptions.** The share of terms with a description.
- **Metadata.** Whether it includes a title, description, license and version.
- **Reuse.** How other ontologies in the catalog depend on it.

We will continue gathering feedback on scoring and which signals to include. We also plan to offer
additional, context-dependent measures for advanced users.

An ontology may have no seal because it has not been scored or has not met the current criteria.

## Quality details

When available, open **Quality details** on the ontology's page to see its assessment.
